Lasagna

A picture of a slice of my favorite Lasagna

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Lasagna Noodle Plates
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Bechamel Sauce
  • Other stuff...

Steps

  1. Cook minced meat
  2. Cook Tomato Sauce
  3. Cook Bechamel Sauce
  4. Do layers in following order:
    1. Minced meat
    2. Tomato Sauce
    3. Lasagna Noodle Plates
    4. Minced Meat
    5. Tomato Sauce
    6. Lasagna Noodle Plates
    7. Bechamel Sauce
    8. Cheese
  5. Cook for 60min @180°C
